# SXO audit, 2026-10-01

Scope: the live site (35 URLs; the build in `dist/` was byte-identical to live apart from one chatbot
timestamp), checked on desktop and at **375 px (phone)**. The Start Project form was opened and its client-side
validation exercised **without sending anything**.

**What I could not assess:** actual visitor behavior. I have no GA4 access (funnels, scroll depth, form
abandonment) and no session recordings, so every finding below comes from structure, measurement of the pages
themselves and standard usability criteria, not from observed user behavior. Priorities are judgment calls.

Implemented in commit `5d7768d` (branch `seo/sxo-pass`). Nothing deployed.

## Implemented (clear-cut)

| # | Finding (evidence) | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Service pages had no CTA near the top.** The only "Start a Project" in the page body was the last element (99% down). On a phone the header button lives inside the hamburger menu, so the first visible CTA was at **3,700-6,400 px** down (GEO 5,123; SEO 6,423; DevOps 3,925). | Hero CTA on all 11 service pages, service pre-selected in the form. Now **441 px** on `/services/seo/` (above the fold). Plus a link to the real case study where one exists (SEO, eBook, DevOps, Cloud, Hardware only; none invented). |
| 2 | **Articles: first CTA 11,000-26,000 px down** on a phone (longest: 26,347 px), and no path to a service page except at the end. | "Related services" line near the top of every article (links to the matching service pages). |
| 3 | **Long articles had no way to scan or jump** (up to 15 sections, 3,600 words). | Collapsible "In this article (N sections)" list for articles with 4+ sections; anchors verified to clear the fixed header. |
| 4 | **Homepage had no proof**: no link to case studies or the Team Track Record in the page body. | "See the Work Behind the Claims": two cards linking the case studies and the Team Track Record. **No numbers or quotes added**; the framing repeats the Track Record page's own honest wording ("before byteandbook.com existed as a business site"). |
| 5 | **FAQ questions were 24 px tall tap targets** on phones. | Padding moved so the whole row (about 64-88 px) is tappable; visual spacing unchanged. |
| 6 | **Bug (mine, from 2026-09-27): `/privacy/` scrolled sideways on phones** (392 px wide at 375 px) because the long Google URLs I added did not wrap. | Links now break; verified 375 px, no overflow. |

QA now guards each of these (2,741 assertions, 0 failures), and I confirmed the guards fail when the fix is removed.

## What already works well (keep)

- One H1 per page and no skipped heading levels on any of the 35 pages.
- No horizontal overflow on the other 29 pages tested at 375 px (30 tested in total).
- Category pages and the homepage already have a CTA in the first screen (460-620 px on a phone).
- Start Project form: opens with the first field focused, **pre-selects the service you came from**, shows
  "Please fix 6 fields below", moves focus to the first problem, sends nothing while invalid, and states
  "does not place an order or create a payment obligation" next to the submit.
- Team Track Record: stats are computed in code from the data (31 reviews, mean 4.855, shown as 4.9) and the
  videos use `preload="none"`, so ~20 MB is not loaded until played.
- FAQ blocks on all 14 service/category pages; Team Track Record and case studies linked from `/work/`.

## Flagged: judgment or business calls (not implemented)

| # | Finding | Why it is not clear-cut | My recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | **Phone number is a required field**, while "Preferred contact method" allows Email only. | Sales may want phones; a required phone is a common drop-off for B2B forms. | Make it optional unless sales truly needs it. Your call. |
| B | **Form is about 2.5 screens tall on a phone** (13 fields; 6 required including two consent checkboxes). | The consent boxes are legal; the optional fields are your intake process. | Consider a short first step (name, email, what you need) and the rest optional. Legal review for merging the two checkboxes. |
| C | **No response-time expectation** on the form, success message or contact page. | Needs a true commitment from you; I will not invent one. | Give me a real number ("we reply within 1 business day") and I will add it. |
| D | **No persistent CTA on phones** (the header button is inside the menu). | A sticky bar competes with the floating "Ask ByteAndBook AI" button; a design decision. | Test a slim sticky "Start a Project" bar on service pages, and move or restyle the chat launcher. |
| E | **Modal does not lock background scroll** on phones (`body` overflow stays visible). | Low risk, but it is a change to the project-request flow's script. | Small fix; say go and I will do it. |
| F | **Team Track Record videos have no captions or transcripts.** | I cannot caption audio I do not have; accessibility and conversion both benefit. | Provide transcripts and I will add captions/track elements. |
| G | **Reviewer names and companies are published** (first name + initial, company as on the platform). | Permission/terms question, already open with you. | Needs your explicit clearance. |
| H | **About page has no named people** (leadership list is empty). B2B visitors look for faces. | Only you can say who may be named and what may be said. | Elizabeth Luna is already approved as an author; a short, approved bio for her (and anyone else) would fix this. |
| I | **Long paragraphs in articles** (up to 207 words in `devops-for-startups`; 5 paragraphs over 90 words in `geo-vs-seo`; the longest stretch between any two headings is 458 words in `how-to-get-cited-by-ai`). | Editing the other author's copy is an editorial call. | Break up the longest paragraphs and add a subhead or two; I can propose edits for review. |
| J | **Case studies and Team Track Record pages are long (about 5,800-11,200 px on a phone) with a CTA only at the end.** | Mid-page CTAs are a design choice. | A single mid-page "Start a Project" after the first proof block. |
| K | **Footer and breadcrumb links are about 20 px tall** (WCAG 2.2 asks for 24 px or spacing). | Footer layout/design. | Add vertical padding to footer links. Minor. |

## Suggested order

C (needs only a number from you) > A/B (form) > D (sticky CTA) > H (named people) > I/J > E/F/K.
