ByteAndBook supplies any type of computer hardware (components, workstations,
servers, networking gear and peripherals) and ships it anywhere in the
United States. You place the order; we source it and deliver it to your
doorstep.

Our prices are set below the standard market and online rate for the same
hardware. We don't publish a flat discount figure, because the gap depends
on the item and on current supply, so ask for a quote and compare it with
what you're seeing elsewhere.

If you'd rather not choose parts yourself, recommendations start from the
workload, whether that's rendering, inference, build pipelines, or hosting,
and work backward to a specified, balanced configuration rather than a
generic parts list.
